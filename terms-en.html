Formation and duration

An agreement is formed as soon as the order confirmation has been accepted by both parties in writing or by e-mail. This expressly does not apply to the response and proposal periods we state on our website: that you receive a response within four hours on working days and a substantiated proposal within 48 hours of the inspection is our commitment. If we fail to meet it, please let us know.

Fees and payment

Fees are communicated transparently in advance and are exclusive of VAT, unless stated otherwise. To consumers we communicate fees inclusive of VAT. Invoices must be paid within fourteen days of the invoice date. In the event of late payment, the other party is in default without notice of default being required, and statutory (commercial) interest and extrajudicial collection costs in accordance with the Dutch Extrajudicial Collection Costs (Standards) Act (Wet normering buitengerechtelijke incassokosten) are payable. Additional work is charged only where it has been agreed in advance or logically follows from the assignment.

Notice and termination

Agreements for an indefinite period may be terminated by either party in writing subject to a notice period of one calendar month, effective at the end of a month. For an assignment for a specific project, the agreement ends upon completion. Either party may terminate the agreement with immediate effect if the other party has been declared bankrupt, has been granted a suspension of payments or ceases its business. Obligations which by their nature continue — such as confidentiality and liability — remain in force after termination.

Liability

HomeINN undertakes to carry out its work with due care. Liability is limited to direct loss demonstrably arising from an attributable failure. In all cases liability is limited to the amount paid out by HomeINN's liability insurer in the case concerned, plus the excess. If the insurer does not pay out, liability is limited to the amount invoiced for the assignment concerned over the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the loss. Liability for indirect loss, including consequential loss, lost profit and lost savings, is excluded. These limitations do not apply in the event of intent or deliberate recklessness on the part of HomeINN or its managers.

Force majeure

If HomeINN is unable to perform its obligations owing to a circumstance not attributable to it — such as government measures, failures at suppliers, extreme weather conditions or the failure of utilities — those obligations are suspended for as long as the force majeure lasts. If the force majeure lasts longer than sixty days, either party may terminate the agreement in respect of the unperformed part, without any obligation to pay compensation arising.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied, please report this within a reasonable time after you have discovered the defect or could reasonably have discovered it, by e-mail to info@homeinn.nl; for consumers, notification within two months is in any event timely. We confirm receipt within five working days and aim to respond substantively within four weeks. A complaint does not suspend the payment obligation.

Consumers' right of withdrawal

If, as a consumer, you conclude an agreement for a service with HomeINN at a distance (for example via this website or by telephone), you have the statutory right to withdraw from that agreement within fourteen days without giving reasons. If you ask us to start the service within that period, you pay a proportionate amount for the work already carried out. The right of withdrawal does not apply to the purchase or sale of real estate.
