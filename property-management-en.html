How does the minimum fee work?

With a basic rent of € 1,200 per month, Package 2 works out at 8% = € 96. Because this is lower than the minimum fee of € 135, the minimum fee applies. Once the rent exceeds € 1,688 per month, the percentage becomes higher than the minimum fee and the percentage applies. You always pay the higher of the two — but never both at once. This is shown transparently on your report every month.

Frequently asked questions

What owners
ask us.

What does property management at HomeINN cost?
You pay a fixed percentage of the basic rent: 5% (Core Management) or 8% (Full Operational), with a minimum fee of € 95 and € 135 per unit per month respectively (excl. VAT). You always pay the higher of the percentage or the minimum fee — never both. Use the calculator above for your situation.
What is the difference between the three packages?
Core Management covers the administration (collection, agreements, reporting). Full Operational adds complete technical and maintenance management, with a 24/7 fault line and SLA response times. Performance Partner is for owners who want to grow actively: we co-invest in improvement projects and share in the result.
Am I tied to a long contract term?
No. We believe in management that proves itself. You receive a clear agreement with a short, reasonable notice period — no multi-year commitment to keep you in.
Do you also manage a single home?
Yes. Whether you let one home or run a complete portfolio — both are welcome. The minimum fee per unit ensures that even a single home is managed profitably and carefully.
How quickly are faults resolved?
With Full Operational and Performance Partner a 24/7 fault line applies with agreed response times (SLA); the exact times are set out in your management agreement. Emergencies are dealt with immediately; routine maintenance is scheduled in consultation with tenant and owner.
Can I switch packages later?
Always. The packages build on each other, so scaling up from Core to Full Operational or moving on to Performance Partner is possible at any time — without any hassle.
