Our fixed construction partner

We promise nothing our fixed construction partner cannot build.

A renovation is only as strong as the hands that carry it out. That is why HomeINN does not work with a changing cast of subcontractors, but with one fixed construction partner: Lageweg Services B.V. — a versatile construction and maintenance company from the Rotterdam region, specialising in renovation and full-service maintenance for private clients, owners' associations (VvE) and commercial clients.

Every HomeINN project — from façade repair to a complete home renovation — is prepared, costed and carried out together with this team. One point of contact, well-attuned tradespeople and a shared standard: quality, reliability and sustainable solutions. So you know in advance who will be on the scaffolding — and that the result matches the budget.

"Building satisfaction together."
— Lageweg Services B.V.
