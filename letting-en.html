Have a home to let?

We find and screen the right tenant and handle the entire process — from listing to handover of keys. After that you can move on to full property management.

  • Listing on the major rental platforms (including Pararius) and on our own site
  • Full screening: income requirement of 3× the rent, identity and credit check and references — this is how we handle that data
  • Tenancy agreement in line with Rotterdam and national regulations
  • Viewings and handover of keys fully arranged
