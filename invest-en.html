Taking part in our projects.

How it works

From introduction to payout.
In three steps.

01
Personal introductionWe get to know each other and discuss your goals, the amount available and the term that suits you.
02
Project information & agreementYou receive the full figures: purchase, refurbishment budget, schedule and the return percentage — for a one-year term a target return of 7% per year — set out in a private agreement.
03
Follow & payoutAs soon as the refurbishment starts you receive a progress update by e-mail; the status of each property is shown on the projects page. On sale, your deposit plus the agreed return is paid out via the civil-law notary.
