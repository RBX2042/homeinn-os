Our portfolio

Eleven properties. Thirty-three apartment rights.

Our portfolio in Rotterdam and Vlaardingen consists of eleven properties which, once the plans are carried out, will form 33 self-contained apartment rights. The strategy: add value through subdivision, re-planning and high-end full renovation, followed by the sale or letting of each home individually.

11 properties · 33 apartment rights · in our own portfolio · as at September 2026

Project vision & renovation standard

The portfolio is being transformed into high-end, fully modernised and energy-efficient homes for the upper segment of the private rental and owner-occupier market.

  • Complete full renovation — strip and renew while retaining usable structural elements.
  • High-end finishing — luxury kitchens, modern bathrooms and quality floor, wall and ceiling finishes.
  • New installations — electrics, heating, ventilation, water and technical services are modernised.
  • Energy-efficient & future-proof — the highest achievable energy label per home and, where technically possible, gas-free.
  • Modern living comfort — smart layouts, good natural light and optimal use of every square metre.
  • Turn-key completion — fully finished and ready for sale or letting in the upper market segment.

From existing property to sustainable, high-end and future-proof homes.

Phased sale or letting of each individual apartment right, depending on market conditions and strategy.

Objective & exit strategy

The objective is to create high-end self-contained homes within each property that can be sold and financed individually. The portfolio can be sold in phases per apartment right. Individual homes can also be retained for letting, or refinancing can be considered once the renovations are complete. The final route is decided per property, based on the market conditions at that time.

Important considerations

What you as an investor should bear in mind.

  • Market riskThe value after renovation remains a valuation/estimate and may differ from the sale price actually achieved.
  • Construction riskSchedule, availability and costs of the renovation may change during execution.
  • Financing riskInterest rates, terms and refinancing options may change.
  • Subdivision & permitsExecution remains dependent on the required legal, structural and municipal conditions.
  • LiquidityProperty is not immediately liquid; the timing and duration of a sale differ per property.
