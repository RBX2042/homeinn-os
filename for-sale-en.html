Why a HomeINN home

Surveyed, refurbished,
no surprises.

Because we buy our homes ourselves and refurbish them with our fixed construction partner, we know exactly what is behind the walls. Installations, insulation and finishing meet our own standard — and we record that in a structural survey. You buy move-in ready, with no surprises. The sale itself runs through our estate agent; we remain the developer and the party that knows the property.
