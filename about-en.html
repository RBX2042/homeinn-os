About HomeINN · Rotterdam

Developer in Rotterdam.

Founded by people who know the Rotterdam property market inside out — developer, buyer and manager under one roof. With a fixed team and a fixed construction partner.

What we stand for

Six values,
one way of working.

01 — Transparency

No small print

Fixed percentages, a clear minimum fee and monthly insight into every cent. You always know exactly what HomeINN costs and what it delivers.

02 — Reliability

Agreements are sacred

One fixed contact who knows your property, available on working days from 08:00 to 18:00 — and for emergencies the 24/7 fault line from Full Operational. No call centre, no queues. What we promise, we do.

03 — Results

Your success is our success

With Performance Partner we earn a share of what you earn extra. That is no coincidence — it is the core of how we have designed our services.

04 — Local knowledge

Rotterdam inside out

We know the rents per district, the consequences of the Affordable Rent Act (Wet betaalbare huur) and every good maintenance firm in the region. Local knowledge cannot be bought — it is built.

05 — Partnership

More than a manager

We advise, invest alongside you and grow with your portfolio. That is why we think in years, not in separate assignments.

06 — Compliance

Regulation actively followed

The WWS points system, the Affordable Rent Act, the Rotterdam landlord permit — we actively monitor the regulations and adjust your management in consultation with you.

Our story

Rotterdam had no property partner that steps in itself. So we built one.

Most parties choose one role: the agent sells, the contractor builds, the manager collects the rent. HomeINN grew out of the conviction that it works better when those roles sit under one roof — with our own direction instead of loose links.

We ask nothing of owners that we do not do ourselves every day in our own portfolio: buying, developing, letting, managing and selling at the right moment. That experience — gained on the scaffolding, at the notary and at the kitchen table with tenants — is exactly what we put to work for you.

The journey

Almost twenty years building on one conviction.

HomeINN did not come about behind a desk, but on the scaffolding, at the notary and at the kitchen table with tenants. This is the road that led here.

2007

The first purchase

It starts small: one home, bought with a clear vision. Carefully refurbished, held for years and eventually sold with a demonstrable return. The lesson that shapes everything since: you create value yourself — through selection, maintenance and patience.

2014

From one home to a portfolio

The approach becomes a family business. In the years that follow, more than twenty properties in Rotterdam and surroundings are bought, refurbished, let and sold. Every phase of the property cycle — due diligence, renovation, tenant management, sale timing — is learned in practice, property by property.

2020

Harvesting and reinvesting

A series of successful sales confirms the strategy: buy at the right moment, add value, sell with results. The proceeds do not drain away but are systematically reinvested in new projects, energy upgrades and a growing rental portfolio with stable cash flows.

2025

HomeINN is born

Almost twenty years of practical experience are brought together in one professional organisation: HomeINN B.V., based in Rotterdam. Our own portfolio, management in our own hands, clear governance and reporting — the informal strength of a family business, in the structure that owners and financiers deserve.

Now

One house, five disciplines

Today HomeINN buys, develops, sells, lets and manages under one roof — with well over a thousand square metres of living space under active management and a portfolio of its own that grows steadily. We ask nothing of owners that we do not do ourselves every day in our own portfolio. That is not a slogan. That is the journey.

We promise nothing our fixed construction partner cannot build.

We carry out every project with one fixed construction partner — Lageweg Services B.V. One point of contact, a well-attuned team of tradespeople and quality within budget.
