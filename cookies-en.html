Cookie policy

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers. Only your own browser stores form data locally; maps load only after you click.

This English translation is provided for convenience. In the event of any discrepancy, the Dutch version (cookiebeleid) prevails.

Version 2.1 · effective date 5 September 2026

No cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. A cookie banner is therefore not required.

Local storage

If you complete a form, your browser also stores the submission locally (localStorage), so that the enquiry is not lost. That local copy remains on your own device. To load the site faster, your browser also keeps a local copy of pages (service worker). That copy, too, remains on your own device. In addition, we send the enquiry itself by e-mail and to our own database, so that we can follow it up — see the privacy policy for who receives those data and how long we retain them.

Third-party scripts

If you touch a form field, your browser loads one script from the content delivery network jsDelivr; in doing so, jsDelivr sees your IP address. No cookie is set. See the privacy policy for details.

Maps

Google Maps maps load only after you click on them. Only at that moment does your browser connect to Google; until then nothing is shared with Google. We load fonts from our own server, not via Google Fonts.
